Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

What we don't collect

Every tool on OmniTools runs entirely in your browser. Files you upload, text you paste, and results you generate are processed on your device and are never transmitted to our servers, because we don't operate a backend that receives that data.

Local storage

Some preferences, like your light/dark theme choice, are saved using your browser's local storage so they persist between visits. This data stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.

Analytics

If we add privacy-respecting analytics in the future (for example, to see which tools are popular), this policy will be updated to describe exactly what's collected.

Third-party links

Pages on this site may link to external resources. We aren't responsible for the privacy practices of sites we don't operate.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out through the contact page.
