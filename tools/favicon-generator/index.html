About this tool

Upload a square image and generate favicons in multiple sizes for your website. Download individual sizes or all at once.

How to use it

  1. Upload a square image (PNG, JPG, or SVG).
  2. Select the sizes you need.
  3. Click Generate & Download to save them all.

Features

  • Generates 16, 32, 48, 180, 192, and 512px sizes
  • Preview before downloading
  • High-quality canvas resizing
  • Client-side processing only

Frequently asked questions

Upload at least 512×512 for best results. The image should be square.

At minimum, use 32×32 for the favicon.ico. Add 180×180 for Apple and 192×192 for Android/PWA.
