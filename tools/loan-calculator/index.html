About this tool

This loan calculator computes your fixed monthly payment using the standard amortization formula and generates a full payment schedule showing how much of each payment goes toward principal vs. interest over the life of the loan.

How to use it

  1. Enter the total loan amount in dollars.
  2. Enter the annual interest rate (APR) as a percentage.
  3. Choose the loan term in years (e.g. 5 for a car loan, 30 for a mortgage).
  4. Click Calculate to see your monthly payment, total interest, and the full amortization table.

Features

  • Instant monthly payment calculation
  • Total interest and total cost summary
  • Full amortization schedule with per-payment breakdown
  • Works for auto loans, personal loans, student loans, and more
  • Runs entirely in your browser — no data is sent anywhere
