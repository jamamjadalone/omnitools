About this tool

Analyze any text to determine its overall sentiment — positive, negative, or neutral. Uses keyword-based analysis to score and classify the emotional tone of your text.

How to use it

  1. Paste or type text into the input area.
  2. Click Analyze Sentiment.
  3. Review the sentiment classification, score, and matched keywords.

Features

  • Positive, negative, and neutral classification
  • Numerical sentiment score
  • Visual score bar
  • Shows matched positive and negative keywords

Frequently asked questions

It's a basic heuristic. It works well for straightforward text but may miss sarcasm, context, and nuanced language.

Currently English only, as it relies on an English keyword dictionary.
