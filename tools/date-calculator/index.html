About this tool

A versatile date calculator with two modes: find the exact number of days between any two dates, or add/subtract a specific number of days from a starting date to find a future or past date.

How to use it

  1. Days between: Pick start and end dates to see the difference in days.
  2. Add/Subtract: Choose a base date, select add or subtract, enter the number of days, and see the result.

Features

  • Calculate exact days between any two dates
  • Add or subtract days from a date
  • Handles leap years and month boundaries correctly
  • Instant results as you change inputs
  • Works entirely in your browser
