About this tool

Stuck on what to write? Generate random story prompts combining unique characters, settings, conflicts, and twists across multiple genres to spark your next creative work.

How to use it

  1. Set how many prompts you want.
  2. Choose a genre or leave it on Any.
  3. Click Generate prompts.
  4. Copy your favourites or click Refresh for new ones.

Features

  • Fantasy, Sci-Fi, Horror, Romance, and Mystery genres
  • Random characters, settings, and conflicts
  • Generate 1–20 prompts at a time
  • Quick refresh for instant new ideas
