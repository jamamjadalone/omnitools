Pregnancy Due Date Calculator

Estimate your due date from the first day of your last menstrual period.

Select your LMP date and click Calculate.

About this tool

Estimates the due date by adding 280 days (40 weeks) to the first day of the last menstrual period. This is the standard Naegele's rule used by healthcare providers.

How to use it

  1. Select the first day of your last menstrual period.
  2. Click Calculate Due Date.
  3. View your estimated due date and current progress.

Features

  • Standard Naegele's rule calculation
  • Shows weeks and days pregnant
  • Estimated trimester
  • Progress percentage

Frequently asked questions

Only about 5% of babies are born on their exact due date. The estimate is within 1-2 weeks for most pregnancies. Always consult your healthcare provider.
