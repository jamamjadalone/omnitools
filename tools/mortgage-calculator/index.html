About this tool

This mortgage calculator computes your fixed monthly payment using the standard amortization formula, factoring in home price, down payment, interest rate, and loan term. It also generates a year-by-year amortization summary.

How to use it

  1. Enter the home price and your down payment amount.
  2. Set the interest rate (APR) and choose a loan term.
  3. Click Calculate to see your monthly payment and total costs.

Features

  • Calculates monthly P&I payment
  • Shows total interest over the life of the loan
  • Supports 15, 20, and 30-year terms
  • Yearly amortization summary
  • 100% client-side calculations
