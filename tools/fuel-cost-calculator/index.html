About this tool

This fuel cost calculator divides your trip distance by your vehicle's fuel efficiency (MPG) to determine how many gallons you'll need, then multiplies by the current gas price to estimate total fuel cost.

How to use it

  1. Enter the total trip distance in miles.
  2. Enter your vehicle's fuel efficiency in miles per gallon (MPG).
  3. Enter the current gas price per gallon.
  4. Click Calculate to see your fuel cost estimate.

Features

  • Calculates gallons needed for any trip distance
  • Shows total fuel cost
  • Calculates cost per mile
  • Works for any vehicle type
  • Quick and simple road trip budgeting
