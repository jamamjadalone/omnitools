About this tool

This calculator shows how compound interest works by computing the future value of an investment using the formula A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt), where P is the principal, r is the annual rate, n is compounding frequency, and t is time in years.

How to use it

  1. Enter your starting principal amount.
  2. Enter the annual interest rate as a percentage.
  3. Choose how many years you plan to invest.
  4. Select how often interest compounds (monthly is most common).
  5. Click Calculate to see your future value and year-by-year growth.

Features

  • Supports annual, quarterly, monthly, and daily compounding
  • Shows year-by-year balance and interest breakdown
  • Calculates total interest earned over the full period
  • Ideal for savings accounts, CDs, and investment projections
  • 100% client-side — no data leaves your browser
