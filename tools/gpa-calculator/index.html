About this tool

This GPA calculator computes your cumulative Grade Point Average using the standard weighted formula: sum of (credits × grade points) divided by total credits. It uses the standard 4.0 US grading scale.

How to use it

  1. Enter the course name, credit hours, and select the letter grade.
  2. Click + Add Course to add more rows.
  3. Click Calculate GPA to see your cumulative GPA, total credits, and quality points.
  4. Click the × button to remove a course you don't need.

Features

  • Add unlimited courses
  • Supports standard 4.0 grading scale with +/- grades
  • Shows total credits and quality points
  • Easy add/remove course rows
  • Instant GPA recalculation

Frequently asked questions

It uses the standard US 4.0 scale: A=4.0, A-=3.7, B+=3.3, B=3.0, B-=2.7, C+=2.3, C=2.0, C-=1.7, D+=1.3, D=1.0, D-=0.7, F=0.0.

Pass/fail courses typically don't factor into GPA. You can exclude them by not adding them, or set credits to 0.
