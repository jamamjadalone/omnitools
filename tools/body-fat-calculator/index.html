About this tool

This body fat calculator uses the U.S. Navy method to estimate your body fat percentage based on body circumference measurements. For men, it uses waist and neck measurements with height. For women, it also includes hip circumference.

How to use it

  1. Select your gender and preferred unit (inches or centimeters).
  2. Measure your waist circumference at the narrowest point.
  3. Measure your neck circumference at the narrowest point.
  4. For women, also measure hip circumference at the widest point.
  5. Click Calculate to see your estimated body fat percentage.

Features

  • U.S. Navy method for reliable estimation
  • Supports both male and female measurements
  • Works in inches and centimeters
  • Shows body fat classification category
  • Requires only a tape measure — no special equipment
