Screen Ruler

On-screen pixel ruler overlay to measure elements on your display.

Click "Show Ruler" to display an overlay ruler, or enter a pixel value to draw a fixed-width ruler.

About this tool

A pixel ruler overlay you can drag around your screen to measure elements. The ruler stays on top of the page content so you can visually measure widths and heights of any element.

How to use it

  1. Choose the orientation (horizontal or vertical) and color.
  2. Click Show Ruler to display the overlay.
  3. Drag the ruler by clicking and dragging on it.
  4. Or enter a pixel value and click Draw Manual Ruler to see a ruler of exact width.

Features

  • Horizontal and vertical ruler modes
  • Draggable overlay that stays on top
  • Pixel markings at 10px intervals
  • Multiple color options for visibility
  • Manual measurement mode for exact pixel values

Frequently asked questions
