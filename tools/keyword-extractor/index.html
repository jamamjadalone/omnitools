About this tool

Paste any text and instantly extract the most important keywords based on word frequency. Filters out common stop words and returns the most meaningful terms.

How to use it

  1. Paste your text into the input area.
  2. Choose how many keywords to extract.
  3. Click Extract Keywords.
  4. Copy the keyword list.

Features

  • Frequency-based keyword extraction
  • Automatic stop word filtering
  • Configurable number of results
  • Shows word count for each keyword

Frequently asked questions

It counts word frequency after removing stop words. Words that appear more often are ranked higher.

Common words like "the", "is", "and", "a" that carry little meaning and are filtered out.
