QR Code Reader

Read and decode QR codes from uploaded images.

Upload a QR code image and click Scan.

About this tool

Upload an image containing a QR code and decode its content instantly. Works with URLs, text, contact info, WiFi credentials, and any other QR code data.

How to use it

  1. Upload an image that contains a QR code.
  2. Click Scan QR Code.
  3. The decoded content appears on the right.

Features

  • Decodes standard QR codes
  • Works with any image format
  • Handles URLs, text, and structured data
  • Client-side processing

Frequently asked questions

Try a clearer, higher-resolution image with good contrast. The QR code should be flat and well-lit.

QR codes have error correction. Mild damage can be handled, but severe obstruction may prevent decoding.
