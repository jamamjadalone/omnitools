Edge check tips

  • Zoom to 200–400% and look along the edges for a light or dark outline (halo).
  • Switch between red, green and blue — a colored fringe usually shows leftover background.
  • Check hair, fur and complex edges — they hide fringe the most.

About this tool

__ABOUT__

How to use it

  1. Upload a PNG (or WebP) with transparency using the dropzone.
  2. Switch between the checkerboard and solid color backgrounds to inspect the edges.
  3. Use the zoom slider to inspect edges up close.
  4. Watch for halos, colored fringes or leftover background chunks along the outline.
  5. Fix the cut-out in your editor, re-upload and re-check before submitting.

Features

  • Checkerboard plus white, black, red, green and blue backgrounds
  • Zoom slider for close edge inspection
  • Color mode and transparency stats for your image
  • Warns you when an image has no transparency
  • Runs entirely in the browser

Frequently asked questions

A halo is a light or dark outline around the subject that remains after a rough background removal. It is the most common reason a cut-out looks unprofessional and a frequent cause of stock rejections.
