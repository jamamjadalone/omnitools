About this tool

This retirement calculator projects your future savings based on your current age, retirement age, existing savings, monthly contributions, and expected annual return rate. It accounts for compound growth on both lump sums and regular contributions.

How to use it

  1. Enter your current age and desired retirement age.
  2. Input your current savings and how much you contribute monthly.
  3. Set an expected annual return rate (7% is a common historical average for stocks).
  4. Click Calculate to see your projected nest egg.

Features

  • Projects savings with compound growth
  • Accounts for both lump sum and monthly contributions
  • Shows year-by-year growth breakdown
  • Calculates total contributions vs. interest earned
  • Quick retirement planning overview
