How to use it

  1. Paste your post title into the input.
  2. Choose a separator — hyphen is the recommended standard for URLs.
  3. Optionally strip stop words for shorter slugs.
  4. The result updates instantly.
  5. Copy the slug and paste it into your CMS URL field.

Features

  • Instant slug generation from any title
  • Hyphen, underscore or concatenated output
  • Optional stop-word removal for shorter URLs
  • Accent and special-character handling
  • Example conversions for reference
