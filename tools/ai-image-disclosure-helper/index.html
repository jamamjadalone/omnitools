About this tool

__ABOUT__

How to use it

  1. Choose the platform you are submitting to.
  2. Select how the image was created — fully AI-generated, AI-assisted, or no AI.
  3. Optionally add your own notes describing the AI tools used.
  4. Click Generate disclosure note to get the exact phrasing for that platform.
  5. Copy the note into the disclosure field of your submission and tick the disclosure checkbox if required.

Features

  • Platform-specific disclosure phrasing for Adobe Stock, Shutterstock and Freepik
  • Separate handling for fully generated vs AI-assisted images
  • Custom notes appended to the disclosure
  • A checklist of the disclosure requirements each platform expects
  • Runs 100% in your browser
