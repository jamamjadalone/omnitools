About this tool

This pregnancy calculator estimates your due date by adding 280 days (40 weeks) to the first day of your last menstrual period (LMP). It also determines your current week of pregnancy, which trimester you're in, and your estimated conception date.

How to use it

  1. Enter the first day of your last menstrual period (LMP).
  2. Click Calculate to see your due date and pregnancy details.
  3. View the timeline to see your trimester dates.

Features

  • Estimated due date (EDD) using Naegele's rule
  • Current week and days pregnant
  • Trimester identification
  • Days remaining until due date
  • Estimated conception date
  • Visual pregnancy timeline

Frequently asked questions

Only about 5% of babies are born on their exact due date. Most arrive within a week before or after. The estimate is most accurate when based on an early ultrasound.

First trimester: weeks 1-12, Second trimester: weeks 13-27, Third trimester: weeks 28-40 (delivery).

You can estimate based on when your last period started, or use the conception date minus 14 days as an approximation for LMP.
