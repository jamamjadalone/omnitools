About this tool

This tax calculator estimates your federal income tax using 2024 US tax brackets. It shows how your income is taxed across multiple brackets, your effective tax rate, and your after-tax income. This is an estimate and does not include state taxes, deductions, or credits.

How to use it

  1. Enter your annual gross income.
  2. Select your filing status (Single, Married, or Head of Household).
  3. Click Calculate Tax to see your estimated federal tax and bracket breakdown.

Features

  • 2024 US federal tax brackets
  • Three filing statuses supported
  • Shows tax per bracket breakdown
  • Calculates effective tax rate
  • Shows after-tax take-home income
