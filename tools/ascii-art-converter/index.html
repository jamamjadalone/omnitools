About this tool

Converts your text into large ASCII art banners using block characters. Choose from different styles and fill characters to create eye-catching text art.

How to use it

  1. Enter your text (up to 15 characters).
  2. Choose a style: Block, Shadow, or Banner.
  3. Optionally change the fill character.
  4. Click Convert and copy the result.

Features

  • Three built-in ASCII art styles
  • Customizable fill character
  • Instant conversion
  • Copy-ready monospace output

Frequently asked questions

Use a monospace font to view ASCII art correctly. The output uses a small font size to fit on screen; copy it to a terminal or text editor for best results.

#, @, *, and = are common fill characters. Try different ones for varied looks.
