About this tool

Paste any English text to calculate its Flesch-Kincaid readability scores. Get the Reading Ease score (0–100) and Grade Level to understand how easy your text is to read.

How to use it

  1. Paste your text into the input area.
  2. Click Analyze Readability.
  3. Review the reading ease score, grade level, and text statistics.

Features

  • Flesch Reading Ease score (0–100)
  • Flesch-Kincaid Grade Level
  • Word, sentence, and syllable counts
  • Reading level description

Frequently asked questions

60–70 is considered "standard" and easily understood by 8th–9th graders. Higher scores are easier to read.

It estimates the US school grade level needed to understand the text. Grade 8 = 8th grade reading level.
