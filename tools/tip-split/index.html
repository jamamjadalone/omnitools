Tip & Split Calculator

Split bills with custom items, tip percentages, and per-person totals.

Add items and click Calculate.

About this tool

Split restaurant bills fairly by adding individual items, choosing a tip percentage, and dividing among your group. Great for dining out with friends.

How to use it

  1. Add items with names and prices.
  2. Set the tip percentage with the slider.
  3. Set how many people to split between.
  4. Click Calculate to see per-person totals.

Features

  • Custom item list with prices
  • Adjustable tip percentage (0-50%)
  • Split between 1-20 people
  • Shows subtotal, tip, and per-person total

Frequently asked questions

The result is rounded up to the nearest cent to avoid short-changing anyone.
