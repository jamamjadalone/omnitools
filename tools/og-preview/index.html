About this tool

Enter your Open Graph meta data values to preview how your page will appear when shared on Facebook, LinkedIn, Twitter, and other social platforms.

How to use it

  1. Enter your page title, description, URL, and image URL.
  2. Click Preview to see the social card mockups.
  3. Adjust values to see how different text and images look.

Features

  • Facebook / LinkedIn card preview
  • Twitter card preview
  • Live image preview
  • Manual input for testing before deployment

Frequently asked questions

Facebook caches OG data. Use their Sharing Debugger to clear the cache and re-scrape.

1200×630 pixels is the recommended size for most platforms.
