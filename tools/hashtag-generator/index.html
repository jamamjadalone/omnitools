How to use it

  1. Describe your post topic or paste a short caption.
  2. Choose how many hashtags you want (3–30).
  3. Optionally enable CamelCase for multi-word tags.
  4. Click Generate hashtags.
  5. Copy the ready-to-paste list and add it to your post.

Features

  • Template-based hashtag generation from a built-in topic dictionary
  • Customizable count from 3 to 30
  • CamelCase option for readability
  • Relevance scoring based on your input words
  • 100% on-device

Frequently asked questions
