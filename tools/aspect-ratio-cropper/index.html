About this tool

__ABOUT__

How to use it

  1. Pick the target aspect ratio (square, 16:9, stock squares and more).
  2. Upload your image.
  3. Drag inside the crop area to position the crop frame over the best part of the image.
  4. Choose the output format (JPG, PNG or WebP).
  5. Click Download crop to save the result.

Features

  • Presets for 1:1, 4:3, 3:2, 16:9, 9:16 and 2:3
  • Freeform mode for any ratio you type
  • Drag-to-position crop with live preview
  • JPG, PNG and WebP output
  • Processed entirely in your browser
