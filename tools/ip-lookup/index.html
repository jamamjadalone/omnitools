IP Lookup

Display your current public IP address and approximate location details.

Click "Look up my IP" to see your public IP address and location.

About this tool

This tool fetches your public IP address and displays it along with approximate location, ISP, and other network details using the free ip-api.com service.

How to use it

  1. Click Look up my IP.
  2. Your public IP address and details appear in the output panel.
  3. Click Copy to copy the IP to your clipboard.

Features

  • Shows public IP address
  • Approximate city, region, and country
  • ISP and organization name
  • Timezone information

Frequently asked questions

This shows your public IP address as seen by external servers, not your local network (private) IP.

Location is approximate, typically at the city level. It's based on the ISP's registered address, not your exact physical location.
