About this tool

This age calculator computes your precise age down to the day, accounting for varying month lengths and leap years. It also shows total days lived and fun extras like your zodiac sign.

How to use it

  1. Select your date of birth using the date picker.
  2. Optionally set a reference date (defaults to today).
  3. Click Calculate Age to see your precise age breakdown.

Features

  • Exact age in years, months, and days
  • Total days, weeks, and hours lived
  • Days until next birthday
  • Zodiac sign lookup
  • Custom reference date support
