About this tool

This discount calculator computes the sale price by multiplying the original price by (1 - discount/100). It also shows exactly how much you save and the final price you'll pay.

How to use it

  1. Enter the original price of the item.
  2. Enter the discount percentage (e.g. 20 for 20% off).
  3. Click Calculate to see the sale price and savings.

Features

  • Instant sale price calculation
  • Shows exact dollar savings
  • Supports any discount percentage from 0-100%
  • Works with decimal prices and percentages
  • Great for shopping and budgeting
