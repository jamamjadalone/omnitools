About this tool

Upload an image and drag to select a crop area. Use preset aspect ratios or crop freely. Download the cropped result as PNG.

How to use it

  1. Upload an image.
  2. Drag on the image to define the crop area.
  3. Choose an aspect ratio preset or use freeform.
  4. Click Crop & Download to save.

Features

  • Freeform or constrained aspect ratio cropping
  • Visual crop overlay with darkened areas
  • Preset ratios: 1:1, 4:3, 16:9, 3:2, 9:16
  • One-click download of cropped image
