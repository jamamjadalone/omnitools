About this tool

Rewrite stiff or formal text to sound more natural. Replaces overly complex words, simplifies sentence structures, and adds a conversational tone based on your chosen formality level.

How to use it

  1. Paste your text into the input area.
  2. Choose a formality level.
  3. Click Humanize and review the output.

Features

  • Replaces formal words with simpler alternatives
  • Simplifies complex sentence structures
  • Three formality levels
  • Preserves original meaning
