About this tool

__ABOUT__

How to use it

  1. Pick the month for your calendar.
  2. Choose the main platform and the content theme for the month.
  3. Click Generate calendar.
  4. Preview the CSV in the right panel — every day of the month is a row.
  5. Download it and open in Excel, Google Sheets or your planner.

Features

  • Full month of daily content slots
  • Platform and theme fields applied to every row
  • Pre-built columns: date, platform, theme, topic, caption, media, status, notes
  • Copy or download as .csv
  • Client-side generation
